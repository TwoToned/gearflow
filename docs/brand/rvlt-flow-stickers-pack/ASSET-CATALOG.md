## Known design choices

- The 3-pin/XLR cable and par can were retired. The monitor and moving head belong to the current collection.
- The microphone uses the simplified stage-microphone silhouette without sound rays.
- The current coffee mug uses shape, rim, handle and steam; there is no bolt.
- The walkie-talkie uses the thinner body. Do not widen it to fill its sticker.
- Curtains frame the interior and are the deliberate exception to ordinary central icon sizing.
- Show time is a taller oval. Preserve its aspect ratio and compare its visible weight with adjacent patches.
- RVLT Flow is an expressive Archivo and Baloo 2 artwork lockup. The main app logo remains separate.
- The pelican is the bird. It is not a Pelican equipment case or an Albert character asset.
